Instructions

The Goal

The goal of minesweeper is to remove all the squares without bombs on them. If you click a square with a bomb on it you lose.

The controls

To remove a square, click it. To place a flag, right click on the square, the square will turn blue.

How to Play

Start by clicking a random square. The number revealed (hopefully you did not click a bomb, although it is posible) tells you how many bombs are around that square including diagonally. If you want to place a flag to mark a bomb, right click on the square.

Example



In this example, we know that there is a bomb on the square with the flag because of the bold number '1' in the upper left of the example field. The number '1' tells us there is one bomb around the square and all other squares are removed except for one. Therefore, the last remaining square must be a bomb. We can now analyze the number to the right of the bold number '1'. Because the location of the bomb touching this square is already known, all other squares around this square can be removed.

This logic can be used for other numbers as well. If you found all the bombs around a '3' square than any remaining unclicked squares do not have bombs on them.
